Important Information

Program terms & conditions.

Please review the course rules before enrolling.

What happens when the administrator postpones a lesson?

If the lesson administrator is delayed, the session will be rescheduled to a suitable time communicated to all affected learners.

What happens if I miss a lesson?

You will need to attend the relevant lesson when it is taught to the next group. A R150 make-up lesson fee will apply.

Can I restart a chapter?

Restarting a chapter is subject to the original applicable chapter fee of R100 or R250. Confirm the exact charge before restarting.

Can I skip chapters?

Chapters are designed to be completed in sequence. The advanced option is intended for learners who already meet the foundational knowledge requirements.

Are course payments refundable?

The stated program policy is that payments are non-refundable. Any refund restriction is subject to applicable consumer protection laws and statutory consumer rights.

Will I make money after completing the program?

Financial trading carries a risk of substantial losses, including loss of your entire trading capital. Educational training cannot eliminate these risks but we set out a goal to reduce it.
